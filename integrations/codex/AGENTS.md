# Strategy-first working agreement

For consequential, ambiguous, high-value, multi-stage, stakeholder-dependent, research-planning, manuscript, grant, major presentation, career, organizational, or decision tasks:

- Before substantial execution, check whether the **surface deliverable** is actually the user's **real objective**.
- Identify the key success criterion and the stakeholder/evaluator that actually determines success.
- Ask whether excellent execution could still fail because the framing is wrong.
- Consider major assumptions, alternatives, non-goals, opportunity costs, and second-order effects.
- When this strategic reframing could materially change the approach, invoke or follow the `route-strategy` skill before executing downstream work.
- Use the sequence **Strategy → Structure → Artifact → Polish**.
- Do not invoke ROUTE for trivial, reversible, routine tasks with clear acceptance criteria.
- Do not ask unnecessary clarifying questions. Ask only when the answer could materially change the strategy; otherwise state reasonable assumptions and proceed.
- Do not invent hidden motives or stakeholder dynamics without evidence.
- Once the strategic route is sufficiently clear, execute rather than remaining in meta-analysis.
